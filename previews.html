Hollow Knight: Silksong — We Played Four Hours. Finally.

After six years of waiting, Team Cherry's sequel is more than a follow-up — it's a complete reimagining of what a Hollow Knight game can be. Hornet controls faster and more fluidly than Grimm. The combat has an aggression and rhythm the original deliberately avoided. The biomes we saw — three of an apparent eight — are among the most visually inventive in the series.

Elden Ring Nightreign Beta — FromSoftware's Co-op Gamble Pays Off

Three players, a condensed Elden Ring map, escalating threat, and a final boss drawn from the lore you've half-remembered since 2022. After three hours in the open beta, Nightreign feels like the extraction roguelite FromSoftware was always going to make eventually. The question was always when, not if.
